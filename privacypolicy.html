OPVRC Map Bot

Privacy Policy • Effective January 2025

1. Data Collection

We collect minimal data necessary for bot functionality:

  • Discord User IDs: For command responses and owner verification
  • Interaction Logs: Basic command usage for debugging
  • Public Territory Data: From community Google Sheets
We do not: Sell your data, store messages, or track you across servers.

2. Data Usage

All data is used exclusively for:

  • Displaying accurate territory ownership maps
  • Responding to user commands (/island, /map)
  • Maintaining crew and faction information

3. Data Retention

Owner information is synced periodically from Google Sheets. We retain only current ownership data and remove outdated information automatically.

4. Your Rights

To request data removal or have questions about your information, contact the bot administrators directly through Discord.

5. Changes

This policy may be updated as the bot evolves. Check back periodically for changes.
